How it works

The scene runs entirely in your browser. Country outlines are projected onto the sphere; when you rest the pointer on a region, the app asks the weather API for that area (when your backend is running).

Tip: the sun and moon follow a simple astronomical approximation so lighting and sky feel tied to real time.
